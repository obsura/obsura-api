# Obsura API Non-Goals

This document defines what `obsura-api` is not trying to become in its initial product scope.

These non-goals are deliberate. They keep the first product definition focused on the workflows that matter most.

## 1. Not a Full Enterprise Governance Suite

`obsura-api` is not intended to be a complete governance, compliance, or policy-management platform in its first scope.

It is not responsible for:
- organization-wide DLP governance
- enterprise policy orchestration
- cross-system enforcement
- compliance administration suites

## 2. Not a Universal File-Processing Engine on Day One

The initial product scope is not trying to support every file family.

The first focus remains:
- text
- screenshots
- images
- structured technical text

Broader document and archive support can come later.

## 3. Not a Fully Autonomous AI Sanitizer

Obsura is not being defined as a blind auto-release system.

It is not intended for:
- AI-only decisions without review
- non-reviewable sanitization pipelines
- fully autonomous release of protected content

Review remains central to the product direction.

## 4. Not a Legal-Only Product

Obsura is not being shaped only for law firms, eDiscovery, or formal legal redaction workflows.

Technical and everyday product usage is a primary target, including:
- engineering workflows
- DevOps usage
- incident review
- terminal output sharing
- screenshot sanitization

## 5. Not a Pure Find-and-Replace Utility

Obsura is not just:
- regex replacement
- generic placeholder insertion
- one-shot black-box redaction

It is a reviewable sanitization studio with saved assets, custom detection, and richer transformation behavior.

## 6. Not a General Design Tool

Although the product supports visual masking, overlays, and region transformations, it is not a general graphics or design application.

It is not meant to become:
- a photo editor
- a graphics suite
- a slide tool
- a branding canvas

Visual features must stay in service of protection and safe sharing.

## 7. Not a Monetization-First System

The initial product definition does not include:
- billing systems
- credit systems
- usage-paywall mechanics
- monetization-first marketplace concerns

The primary direction remains open-source and self-hosted friendly.

## 8. Not a Complex Collaboration Platform in V1

The initial scope does not require:
- advanced team permission models
- approval chains
- enterprise routing flows
- complex multi-user collaboration mechanics

The product should work well for a solo user before expanding into heavier collaboration features.

## 9. Not an All-in-One Media Protection Platform Yet

The first scope does not include:
- audio protection
- video protection
- live-stream protection
- broad biometric analysis beyond practical face-region privacy workflows

## 10. Not a Replacement for Every Security Tool

Obsura is not trying to replace:
- DLP platforms
- SIEM systems
- identity systems
- ticketing systems
- document management systems
- every OCR or content-analysis stack

It should support practical workflows, not absorb every adjacent category.

## 11. Not Implementation-Biased in Product Requirements

The product requirements must not hard-code:
- one transport style
- one deployment topology
- one OCR provider forever
- one detection provider forever
- one database engine into the functional definition

Implementation decisions belong in stack, tooling, and delivery documents, not in the functional product contract.

## 12. Not Over-Optimized for Edge Cases Before the Core Works

Obsura must not spend its first product phase chasing:
- rare file formats
- niche compliance demands
- speculative AI features
- extreme polish unrelated to core protection workflows

The first priority is a strong, repeatable core for real daily use.
